The Industrial Standard in Urad Dal Supply

Whole Urad Dal — scientifically Vigna mungo — is a critical industrial raw material for the global food processing industry. Harvested from Chhattisgarh’s fertile plains, our supply represents the pinnacle of agricultural excellence combined with modern milling technology.

At Sahaj Agro, we deliver supply-chain certainty: direct farm-gate procurement, zero aged stock, and no chemical polishing. For B2B buyers this means higher recovery rates and superior functional performance in every manufacturing application.

Food Industry Value

Power-House B2B Ingredient

Urad dal is experiencing renewed demand in plant-based food R&D. Its high protein-to-weight ratio makes it a preferred source for meat-alternative manufacturers.

Its unique starch structure provides critical ‘tackiness’ in gluten-free baking and high-fiber snack manufacturing — an irreplaceable industrial input.

Our sourcing ensures consistent nutritional profiles across harvests, essential for automated standardized production lines.

Export FAQ

Common B2B Queries

What is the MOQ for global export orders?

Our standard Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for international export is one 20ft Full Container Load (FCL), which typically holds 24-25 metric tonnes depending on packaging.

Do you offer private labeling for retail chains?

Yes, we provide comprehensive private labeling services. We can pack in your brand's artwork with custom sizes (500g, 1kg, 2kg, 5kg) for modern retail supermarket shelves.

Which countries do you currently serve?

We actively export to over 15 countries across South East Asia, the Middle East, and Europe, maintaining long-term partnerships with major importers and distribution networks.

What certifications does Sahaj Agro hold?

We are ISO 9001:2015 certified and FSSAI registered. Every export batch undergoes rigorous lab testing to ensure it meets global food safety and purity standards (99.5%+ purity).

What is the standard lead time for shipment?

Typical lead time is 7-14 days from order confirmation to port dispatch, depending on current stock and specific packaging requirements.

Can I request specific moisture levels for the grain?

Yes, we can adjust our processing parameters to meet specific grain moisture requirements (standard is <12%) to ensure stability during long sea transits.
