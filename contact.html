Common Export Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for export? +
For international urad dal exports, our minimum order is one 20ft container (approx. 24 metric tonnes). For domestic wholesale orders within India, we accept shipments starting from 2.5 tonnes.
Do you offer custom private labeling for international buyers? +
Yes, we are a leading private label urad dal supplier. We provide complete white-label packaging services, including custom retail-ready 1kg/5kg pouches and 30kg wholesale bags with your brand identity.
What are your standard export payment terms? +
For global exports, our standard terms are 30% advance via TT and 70% balance against the scanned copy of the Bill of Lading (BL). LC (Letter of Credit) options are available for established, long-term partners.
