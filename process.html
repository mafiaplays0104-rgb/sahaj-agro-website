Export-Grade Manufacturing

Six Steps to 99.5% Purity

A seamless integration of traditional agricultural care and modern optical sorting technology.

01

Ethical Direct Sourcing

Our manufacturing process begins at the roots. We procure raw material directly from over 200 partner farms in Chhattisgarh. By eliminating middlemen, we uphold our foundational values, ensuring farmers receive fair prices while we secure the freshest harvest within 48 hours of reaping.

  • Direct farm-to-mill partnerships
  • Fair trade agricultural practices
  • Premium raw mapte selection
02

Pre-Cleaning & Destoning

Raw grains undergo rigorous mechanical pre-cleaning. Heavy-duty destoners, density graders, and vibrating screens meticulously separate stones, dust, mud balls, and agricultural foreign matter. Only highly cleansed raw material is permitted to enter the main milling line.

  • Automated gravity destoning
  • Industrial dust aspiration
  • Agricultural debris removal
03

Optical Sortex Cleaning

The core of our quality lies in our optical sorting technology. High-resolution cameras analyze every single grain's color and shape. High-speed pneumatic air ejectors instantly remove any discolored, chalky, or damaged grains, guaranteeing a premium sortex-clean product.

  • Advanced optical color sorting
  • Micro-defect rejection
  • Consistent grain size grading
04

Hygienic Milling & Splitting

For our processed varieties, the cleaned whole dal enters our specialized milling units. Using temperature-controlled emery rollers, the skin is gently removed and the grain is split without losing its natural protein and fiber, creating our signature creamy split urad dal.

  • Temperature-controlled de-husking
  • Nutrient-retaining split technology
  • Byproduct separation for cattle feed
05

Export-Ready Automated Packing

The finished product is weighed and packed using fully automated systems to maintain zero human contact. We utilize heavy-duty, moisture-resistant PP and Jute packaging materials designed specifically to lock in freshness during prolonged oceanic transit.

  • Touch-free automated bagging
  • Precision weighing systems
  • Moisture-proof inner lining
06

Container Loading & Dispatch

From our warehouse directly to your destination. We manage seamless stuffing of 20ft and 40ft containers directly at our facility. Paired with complete customs and phytosanitary documentation, our global dispatch process is fast and frictionless.

  • Direct container stuffing
  • Fumigation and pest control
  • Complete export documentation
