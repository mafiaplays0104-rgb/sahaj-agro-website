The Benchmark in Split Urad Dal Supply for Bangladesh

Split Urad Dal (Vigna mungo) is a high-demand industrial raw material in Bangladesh’s lentil processing industry, snack manufacturing sector, and wholesale food distribution network.

Our advanced milling facility in Chhattisgarh processes premium whole black gram into uniformly split, export-calibrated dal specifically tailored for Bangladesh import requirements.

We operate a direct farm-gate procurement model, eliminating intermediaries and ensuring:

  • Consistent batch quality
  • Controlled moisture levels for sea transit
  • Zero chemical polishing
  • Higher recovery rates for Bangladeshi processors
  • Stable fermentation performance

With structured export documentation and Bangladesh-compliant shipments, we deliver secure containerized supply through Chittagong Port with full traceability and laboratory-certified assurance.
