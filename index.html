Distributor & Exporter FAQ

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for export?
For wholesale pricing in India, our starting MOQ is 25 tonnes. For international shipping, we operate as a container load urad dal supplier, typically starting at one 20ft container (approx. 24 metric tonnes).
Do you provide FSSAI certified urad dal?
Yes, all our products are strictly processed under FSSAI and ISO-certified conditions, ensuring they meet rigorous international food safety and hygiene standards.
Can you act as a private label urad dal supplier?
Absolutely. We offer comprehensive white-labeling and private label packaging services for retail chains and global distributors. Minimum volumes apply.
What export packaging options do you offer?
We utilize strong, export-grade moisture-safe packaging. Our standard urad dal export packaging is 50kg and 30kg PP/Jute bags, designed to withstand long-distance ocean freight.
What documentation is provided for international shipments?
We provide a complete commercial invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, and third-party Quality Lab Reports to ensure smooth customs clearance.
