US Export FAQ

Common B2B Queries for the US Market

What is the MOQ for US export orders?

Our standard Minimum Order Quantity for the United States is one 20ft Full Container Load (FCL), which yields approximately 24 to 26 metric tonnes.

Is your Cattle Feed compliant with US regulations?

Yes, our feed production adheres strictly to FSMA guidelines and undergoes rigorous laboratory testing to meet all US import and FDA standards.

Can you customize protein levels for specific US dairy herds?

Absolutely. As direct manufacturers, we can adjust Crude Protein (CP) and Total Digestible Nutrients (TDN) ratios to meet the exact requirements of your North American agricultural clients.

What is the typical shipping time to the United States?

Ocean transit from Indian ports to US East or West coast ports generally takes between 30 to 45 days after the container is dispatched.

Do you handle US import documentation?

Yes, our export desk manages FDA Prior Notice, Fumigation Certificates, Phytosanitary paperwork, and COAs to ensure a seamless entry through US Customs.

What is the shelf life of the feed?

Due to our strict moisture calibration (≤ 12.0%) and high-quality packaging, the feed maintains a stable shelf life of 3 to 4 months under proper US warehouse storage conditions.
