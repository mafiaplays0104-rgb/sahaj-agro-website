The North American Benchmark in Split Urad Dal Supply

Split Urad Dal (scientifically Vigna mungo) is a critical industrial raw material for North American food processing, ethnic grocery distribution, and high-protein plant-based formulations. Our advanced milling facility transforms premium whole black gram into uniformly split, precision-graded dal tailored specifically for strict US FDA import standards.

At Sahaj Agro Industries, we operate on a direct farm-gate procurement model. This eliminates intermediaries and ensures consistent batch quality, controlled ocean-freight moisture levels, and zero chemical polishing. For US industrial buyers, this guarantees higher yield recovery and superior functional performance.

Backed by robust US Customs clearance protocols and APEDA registration, we deliver secure containerized shipments meeting East and West Coast compliance thresholds seamlessly. Every container reflects traceable sourcing and laboratory-verified quality assurance.

US Import FAQ

Common Queries from US Buyers

What is the MOQ for Split Urad Dal export to the US?

Our minimum order is 1 x 20ft Full Container Load (FCL), which typically holds roughly 24 metric tonnes depending on the packaging chosen.

Is your Split Urad Dal FDA and FSMA compliant?

Absolutely. We rigorously follow all FSMA guidelines and ensure our grains fall well below FDA defect action levels, with multi-stage sortex cleaning and lab verification.

Do you supply both Chilka and Washed Split varieties?

Yes, both Chilka (with skin) and Washed (skinless) variants are available with customizable specifications tailored to US industrial requirements.

What are the transit times to the United States?

Depending on the destination port (e.g., Newark vs. Los Angeles), standard sea transit takes approximately 30 to 45 days after the container is dispatched from the Indian port.

Do you provide US customs clearance support?

Yes, we handle FDA Prior Notice submissions and supply all required documentation, including Phytosanitary Certificates, Fumigation Certificates, and COAs, to ensure seamless entry through US Customs.

Do you offer private labeling for US retail chains?

Yes, we provide comprehensive private labeling services. We can pack in your brand's artwork with custom sizes (2lb, 4lb, 10lb) to suit US retail and wholesale supermarket shelves.
