Our Origins

A Leading Urad Dal Mill in Chhattisgarh

In the agricultural heart of India, where the sun kisses the earth with a golden hue, Sahaj Agro Industries was born. It was not merely the inception of a business; it was the sowing of a promise—a promise to deliver uncompromised B2B wholesale authenticity to a market drifting towards artificiality.

Our story begins in the 1990s in Bhatapara, Chhattisgarh, a region globally celebrated for its agricultural richness and prime soil for Mapte (Urad) cultivation. The founders of Sahaj Agro, hailing directly from this very soil, understood that the true essence of food manufacturing lies in purity. They saw an industry flooded with chemically treated, polished, and adulterated pulses that stripped nature of its nutrients to cut costs.

They envisioned a different path—a path of "Sahaj," meaning simple, natural, and effortless. This vision was the seed that would eventually grow into a towering tree of trust, sheltering our partner farmers and nourishing global consumers alike. Today, Sahaj Agro is more than a brand; we are an international export authority and a testament to the power of honest manufacturing.

Manufacturing Legacy

Three Decades of Dal Processing Excellence

The early years were a test of resilience. In an era where mass production and cost-cutting were the norms, choosing the path of zero-adulteration quality was a rebellious act. We started as a modest mill, manually sorting urad dal with a small team of dedicated workers. Every grain was inspected, every wholesale sack was filled with care, and every customer was treated like family.

As the years passed, our roots deepened. The turning point came when we integrated modern technology without compromising our traditional Indian values. We realized that to scale our vision for bulk B2B supply, we needed to marry the wisdom of the past with the innovation of the future. This led to the establishment of our first high-capacity semi-automated processing unit in the early 2000s.

By 2010, Sahaj Agro had become a household name in regional mandis, known for premium quality and consistency. The last decade has been a period of exponential growth, marked by the adoption of cutting-edge Swiss Sortex technology and rigorous FSSAI/ISO certifications. We evolved from being a local favorite to a national manufacturing contender, and eventually, a highly trusted global exporter.

Manufacturing Standards

Precision in Every Grain

Delicacy in export fulfillment requires precision in execution. At Sahaj Agro, our Bhatapara processing facility is a symphony of technology and agricultural tradition.

01

Ethical Raw Sourcing

The manufacturing journey begins at the farm gate. Our procurement experts, with decades of experience, handpick the finest mapte yields in Chhattisgarh. We analyze specific attributes—grain size, moisture content, and color—that define premium quality wholesale urad.

02

The Purification Ritual

Once the raw material enters our facility, it undergoes rigorous mechanical pre-cleaning. We employ a heavy-duty, multi-stage cleaning system that removes stones, dust, and foreign agricultural matter before milling.

03

Advanced Sortex Technology

This is where science meets food safety. Our state-of-the-art Optical Color Sortex machines scan every individual grain. High-speed infrared cameras detect even the slightest discoloration or deformity, rejecting imperfect grains with a precise pneumatic jet of air.

04

Silk Touch Dry Polishing

Unlike conventional Indian dal mills that use heavy oil, water, or leather polish to add artificial shine, we use a unique "Silk Touch" process. This dry polishing technique gently buffs the grain to remove dust while retaining its natural nutrients and authentic flavor profile.

05

FSSAI Lab-Tested Assurance

Before any container is packed, random samples from every batch are tested in our in-house laboratory. We check for cooking time, moisture thresholds (10-12%), and nutritional density. Only when a batch scores a perfect 10/10 does it earn the Sahaj Agro export seal.

Agricultural Sustainability

Empowering the Hands that Feed

An export business is only as strong as the earth that supplies it. Our corporate growth is intrinsically linked to the prosperity of our farmers. Chhattisgarh is widely known as the "Rice Bowl of India," but its potential for high-protein pulses is equally immense. We work directly with over 500 partner farmers, empowering them with knowledge about sustainable farming practices.

We actively encourage crop rotation, which not only preserves soil health but also ensures a significantly better yield of urad dal. By eliminating middlemen and brokers, we ensure that our farmers receive fair, above-market pricing for their hard work. Furthermore, our manufacturing plant is designed to be highly eco-conscious, strictly adhering to zero-waste policies where milling by-products are naturally processed into high-protein cattle feed.
